What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, applications and sensitive information from cyber threats.

As our lives become increasingly connected to technology, cybersecurity plays a critical role in protecting personal data, business operations and online services.

🛡 Personal Security

Protects personal information from theft.

💳 Financial Protection

Prevents fraud and financial loss.

🏢 Business Security

Protects organizations and digital assets.

🌐 Digital Trust

Supports safe online communication.

What is a Cyberattack?

A cyberattack is an attempt to gain unauthorized access, steal information or disrupt digital systems.

🎯 Steal Data

Passwords, personal data and company records.

🔓 Gain Access

Entering systems without permission.

💥 Disrupt Services

Stopping systems from working normally.

💰 Financial Gain

Making money through cybercrime.

🧠 Social Engineering

Social engineering is a cyberattack technique that manipulates people into revealing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

Instead of attacking computers directly, attackers exploit human emotions such as trust, fear, urgency, and curiosity.

🤝 Trust

People naturally trust familiar organizations and brands.

⚠ Fear

Attackers create panic to force quick decisions.

⏰ Urgency

Victims are pressured to act immediately.

🎁 Curiosity

Interesting files or rewards attract attention.

Let's explore the most common types.

📧 Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering attack that uses fake emails, websites, or messages to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

Example Scenario

From: support@university-security.com

To: nida.gozutok@student.edu.tr

Subject: Urgent Account Verification Required

Dear Nida,

We detected unusual activity on your university account. Please verify your account within 24 hours.

⚠ Why is this suspicious?

  • Creates urgency
  • Unknown sender
  • Requests sensitive information
  • Encourages immediate action

📱 Smishing

Smishing is a phishing attack conducted through SMS messages.

Example Scenario

Hello Nida,

Your package could not be delivered.

Track it here: delivery-check-now.com

⚠ Why is this suspicious?

  • Nida was not expecting a package
  • Suspicious website address
  • Creates urgency
  • Pushes the user to click quickly

📞 Vishing

Vishing uses phone calls to obtain confidential information.

Example Scenario

Caller:

Hello Nida,

We are calling from your bank.

Please provide your verification code.

⚠ Why is this suspicious?

  • Banks do not ask for verification codes by phone
  • Creates fear and panic
  • Pressures the victim
  • Requests confidential information

🎁 Baiting

Baiting exploits curiosity or temptation by offering something attractive.
